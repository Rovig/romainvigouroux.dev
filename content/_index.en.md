I'm **Romain Vigouroux, an AI architect**. I practice agentic coding every day and I design AI systems — agents,
RAG, MCP servers, automation — for business needs that resist off-the-shelf solutions.

My rule: **the need can be complex, the architecture shouldn't be.** Here I write up real cases: the problem, what
made it hard, the options I ruled out, the architecture I chose and what it delivered.
