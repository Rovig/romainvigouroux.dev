**In short: this site sets no cookies and uses no third-party analytics.** Only the server's technical logs are
kept, for 90 days, for security and reading statistics.

## Data controller

Romain Vigouroux, reachable at [contact@romainvigouroux.dev](mailto:contact@romainvigouroux.dev).

## What data, and why

For each page requested, the server records one log line: the IP address, date and time, requested page, referring
page, browser (user agent) and response code.

These logs serve two purposes:

- **security and proper operation of the site** (detecting an outage or abuse);
- **weekly reading statistics**: pages read, estimated number of visitors, visits coming from an AI assistant,
  visits from indexing robots. The report contains only aggregated figures. The number of visitors is estimated
  from a fingerprint of the IP address and browser, computed when the report is produced, different every day and
  never stored: it cannot be used to follow a person from one day to the next.

Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in securing the site and knowing what is read
(Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## How long

Logs are deleted automatically after **90 days** at most. Aggregated weekly reports, without IP addresses, are kept.

## Who has access

Only the publisher. Logs stay on the site's server and are neither sold nor shared.

Traffic passes through **Cloudflare, Inc.**, which provides secure delivery and page caching and, for that purpose,
processes visitors' IP addresses as a processor. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework
and also relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for its transfers. See
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Cookies and trackers

None. The site uses no cookies, no analytics script, no fonts and no content loaded from another site, so there is
no consent banner.

## If you write

A message sent to the contact address is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and follow up.

## External links

Links to LinkedIn, Medium or GitHub lead to sites that apply their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

You can ask to access or erase your data, or object to its processing, by writing to
[contact@romainvigouroux.dev](mailto:contact@romainvigouroux.dev). To find a log line, please give the IP address
and the date of the visit. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL
([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/)).
