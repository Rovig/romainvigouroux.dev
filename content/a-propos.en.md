**Romain Vigouroux is an AI architect.** He designs artificial intelligence architectures — agents, multi-agent
systems, RAG, MCP servers, process automation — that answer complex business needs, and practices agentic coding
every day with tools such as Claude Code.

## What I do

- **Frame the problem before the technology.** Most AI projects fail on a badly framed need, not on the wrong model.
  I start by isolating the constraint that makes the problem hard.
- **Design the simplest architecture that holds.** Fewer components, lower costs, fewer failures.
- **Deliver fast thanks to agentic coding.** I build with coding agents, which lets me prototype and measure in days
  rather than months.
